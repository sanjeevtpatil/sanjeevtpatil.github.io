Research

Research

From precursor chemistry to working device. I study how processing, microstructure and interfaces in oxide–metal nanocomposites set the way charge moves through them, and use that to design sensors that run at room temperature on flexible substrates.

  1. Precursor chemistrySol–gel route from bismuth and iron nitrates
  2. Functional inkOxide particles and silver nanowires in a stable dispersion
  3. Direct writingExtrusion printing in air, with no vacuum step
  4. Percolating networkNanowires mesh around the oxide particles
  5. Flexible deviceWorks flat or bent, at room temperature

Room-temperature gas sensors

Flexible chemiresistors that detect NO₂ and CO₂ at room temperature on bendable plastic. Pairing semiconducting oxides with silver nanowires removes the heater that conventional metal-oxide sensors need.

  • BiFeO₃/Ag sensors for NO₂ (ACS Omega 2024) and CO₂ (Next Materials 2025)
  • TiO₂/Ag dual-gas sensing (Ceramics International 2026) and WO₃/Ag NO₂ sensing (Next Research 2025)
  • Granted Indian Patent 549328

Multiferroic and metal–ceramic nanocomposites

Low-temperature wet-chemical routes to phase-pure bismuth ferrite, and percolation-network design in oxide–metal composites. The network that carries charge in a sensor also attenuates electromagnetic radiation, so one set of design rules spans sensing, optoelectronics and shielding.

  • Percolation-network BiFeO₃ composites as a Faraday cage (Materialia 2025)
  • Sole-authored review of percolation-network design principles (Ceramics International 2026)

Light detection and harvesting

Photoactive oxide films that turn light into a measurable current. Charge separation at oxide–metal and oxide–silicon junctions decides how strongly a device responds, which matters for both photodetectors and solar harvesting.

  • Leaf-inspired flexible, transparent BiFeO₃/Ag photodetectors reaching 530 mA W⁻¹ (Surfaces and Interfaces 2025)
  • Substrate and electrode design for sputtered TiO₂ films, with photocurrent an order of magnitude above earlier TiO₂/Si reports (Solar Energy 2023)

Battery-safety diagnostics

Early warning of thermal runaway in lithium-ion packs under the ANRF MAHA-EV National Mission: gas and temperature sensing, optimal sensor placement, a home-built battery-abuse test platform and reduced-order models of runaway propagation.

  • Three IEEE SEFET 2026 papers on sensor placement and runaway modelling
  • Two patent applications filed in 2026

Printable functional ceramics

Precursor-derived inks, rheology and direct writing as a manufacturing route for oxide devices. Printing is the common thread through my device work: it takes a functional material onto a flexible substrate without vacuum deposition or high-temperature processing.

  • Precursor-based BiFeO₃ ink for direct writing (Materials Letters 2023)
  • Printed transparent BiFeO₃/Ag photodetectors (Surfaces and Interfaces 2025)
  • Printed BiFeO₃/Ag NO₂ sensors on PET (ACS Omega 2024)
  • Direct-ink-written BiFeO₃/Ag CO₂ sensors (Next Materials 2025)
  • BiFeO₃/Ag ink films for EMI shielding (Materialia 2025)
  • Flexible WO₃/Ag NO₂ sensor (Next Research 2025)
  • Direct-written Ag NW–TiO₂ dual-gas sensor (Ceramics International 2026)
